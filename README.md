# Nature Dental Laboratory — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Nature Dental Laboratory Co. manufactures custom dental restorations and appliances for dental professionals in overseas markets. Operating through a Hong Kong office and Shenzhen production laboratory, it provides laboratory production, CAD/CAM fabrication, technical support and export coordination.

The redesigned homepage presents the laboratory’s offering and enquiry action together, improves readability and brings practical ordering information forward.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. An Immediate Enquiry Action

The opening section presents the offering and enquiry button together. Previously, the introduction and contact message appeared on different slideshow slides. Visitors can now understand the laboratory’s services and enquire without waiting for another slide.

### 2. More Readable Navigation and Supporting Text

Larger typography replaces navigation and supporting text previously as small as 13–14px. This makes navigation labels and business information easier to read and scan.

### 3. Earlier Access to Ordering Information

Logistics, turnaround and price-list enquiry information appear earlier, supported by a clear ordering process. Previously, these details were positioned far down the page. The revised layout makes commercial information and ordering steps easier to locate.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual hierarchy:** The offering and enquiry button share the opening section.
- **Typography:** Larger navigation and supporting text improve readability.
- **Layout:** Commercial information appears earlier, with a clear ordering sequence.
- **User experience:** Visitors can move more directly from exploring services to reviewing ordering details and making an enquiry.
