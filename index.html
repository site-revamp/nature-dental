From enquiry to dispatch

A clear route
through the laboratory.

Hong Kong handles incoming cases and outbound dispatch, with production at our Shenzhen laboratory.

Discuss the case

Contact the team about the product, requirements and any questions before sending work.

Send to Hong Kong

Confirm shipment details with the team. Cases arrive at the Hong Kong office for onward coordination.

Production & checks

The laboratory fabricates the work and checks contact points, occlusion, form and shade.

Courier dispatch

Finished work is sent from Hong Kong using your chosen courier arrangements.

Planning your timing?

Most cases need four working days. Complex work may need longer; confirm production and courier timing for your case. Ask for an estimate.
